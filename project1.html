Page to Pillow

A wellness app that helps you wind down for sleep, pairing smart sleep scheduling with personalized book recommendations for better bedtime reading.

Prototype

Overview

We all sleep better when we wind down. We just can't find the time.

Stress, late-night screens, and packed schedules make falling asleep hard. Reading is a proven way to slow down, but finding the right book, and the time to read it, is its own hurdle.

Page to Pillow makes bedtime reading effortless. It calculates your ideal wind-down window from your schedule and sleep goals, then serves up books you'll actually want to open.

Research & Discovery

We started with sleep schedulers like Sleepytime and Rise, then explored wind-down apps like Calm and BetterSleep. One insight reframed the whole project:

Reading is the most effective, research-backed way to wind down.

Unlike scrolling or watching, reading physically slows you down and improves sleep quality. Meditation apps and sleep calculators already existed, but nothing paired sleep scheduling with reading. That gap became Page to Pillow.

Home & Reading Loop

This is the daily home screen for someone already settled into the app. The morning view reports last night's reading as minutes read against a personal reading goal (a lightweight, guilt-free measure of the habit rather than a sleep score the app has no real way to verify). Directly below, it resumes the book you're in the middle of and previews when tonight's wind-down window opens, so the next action is always one tap away.

The heart of the app is the reading loop. In the Books library you browse everything available to read and save titles to lists for later, then choose one as tonight's wind-down book. Once it's set, a notification arrives at your personal wind-down time and takes you straight to the Wind-Down screen, where you can read the book, or switch to its audiobook when your eyes are too tired to read. The reminder is the mechanism that turns "I should read more" into actually opening the book, timed to the exact moment your evening should slow down.

Planned Features

Reading goals & progress

A nightly minutes target with streak-free, low-pressure feedback (the ring/goal the home screen is designed around).

Saved lists & library

Collections for "reading now," "up next," and finished books.

Audiobook mode

Seamless switch between reading and listening within a wind-down session.

Real sleep data

A HealthKit / Health Connect integration to upgrade the morning card's estimated sleep into a measured figure.
